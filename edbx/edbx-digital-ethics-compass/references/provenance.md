## Discrepancies

1. **Centre wording.** SKILL.md: "Put the Human in the Cosmos" (repeated
   throughout, even in the output template). DDC's title and principle: "Put the
   Human at the Center". No "cosmos" on the Principles page. Likely a corruption;
   the output template currently asks every report to carry the wrong phrase.
2. **Structure.** SKILL.md: inner ring of four categories (Data, Avoid
   Manipulation, Make Intentions Transparent, Automation) with middle and outer
   rings. DDC: five principles in the centre; three question categories in the
   outer ring (Automation 10, Data 6, Behavioral design 6 = 22 questions, which
   DDC says are not exhaustive). DDC's "Behavioral design" has no counterpart
   category name in the skill; "Make Intentions Transparent" has no counterpart
   name in DDC (closest: "Make your technology understandable").
3. **Heuristic questions.** Only some of the skill's questions have DDC
   counterparts (data access, anonymisation, profiling, validate-or-challenge,
   algorithm bias, explainability). Others are not on the pages read: data on
   shutdown, who data is shared with, "use people's values against them",
   business incentives visible, algorithmic-curation disclosure, "responsible to
   automate". Label them as the skill's own extension.
4. **Skipped DDC content.** The skill omits Give users control and Avoid
   creating inequality, and omits the DDC automation items on law and human
   rights, job-skill loss, risk, readiness to step in, adaptability and security.
   The skill's own coverage is wider in some places and narrower in others.
5. **Attribution.** SKILL.md names no creator, year or licence. DDC (2021, with
   Peter Svarre) is not credited. Under CC BY, attribution is required if DDC
   text is reproduced.
6. **Steps 0, 0.5, 0.6, status colours, business-pressure attribution and
   implementation types** are not in the source; they are the skill's additions.
7. **"Printable, rotatable wheel"** is not confirmed: the toolkit page calls it
   a circular checklist and gives PDFs.
8. **"Use at every level, from single features to business models"** is not
   found on the pages read.
9. Year: DDC's launch is 2021 (case page); the toolkit file path suggests 2022.

# Provenance — edbx-anti-heroes

Stage A draft (sources and licences, no downloads). One row per element the
skill attributes to a source. Locator and anchor are what could be confirmed
from pages read in this pass; otherwise Locator is `to verify` and the anchor
is marked `(guess)`. Stage B checks each anchor on the page.

Key finding: the primary sources are open and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which blocks
commercial redistribution of verbatim material. The final twelve card names
and their Hero pairs could not be read on any page (they sit in the paper's
card figure, Supplemental Material and the Miro/deck files). Only five
Anti-Hero names appear in the paper text read here, so most of the nine pairs
in SKILL.md and `references/cards.md` are unconfirmed.

## Licence

- **Preprint `mehta2024antiheroes`: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.** We may paraphrase and cite. Reproducing its text or figures needs attribution, non-commercial use only, and any adapted material must be shared under the same licence. The repo is MIT and sold in a paid bundle, so **verbatim reuse of the paper's text or figures is not compatible** with commercial redistribution.
- **Card deck `chivukula2025deck`: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (Chivukula, Mehta, Gray). Same terms. `references/cards.md` reproduces card names and one-line descriptions; if those match the deck verbatim, they are covered by NC and SA, which **blocks commercial redistribution and modification under MIT**. Card names are short, so a bare list of names with attribution is lower risk than the descriptions. Rewriting the descriptions in our own words and citing the deck is the safest route; ship nothing copied from the card faces.
- **DIS 2025 paper `chivukula2025antiheroes`:** ACM publication, licence unknown. Cite only. Check the ACM page for open-access status.
- **Pratt and Miro pages:** no licence stated. Link, do not copy.
- **Gray et al. 2020, 2021:** publisher copyright with repository copies; cite and link only.
- **Universal Methods of Ethical Design:** all rights reserved book; do not reproduce.
- THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md lists Anti-Heroes under "Method sources" only, not under "Material under its own licence". Since the deck and preprint carry CC BY-NC-SA, consider adding them to the licensed-material table.

## Discrepancies

1. **Deck size.** SKILL.md and `cards.md` list 9 Anti-Hero / Hero pairs; the paper says the deck has 12 role cards plus 3 action cards. Three or more cards are missing from the skill deck, yet SKILL.md says "Use only cards from the deck".
2. **FOMO-Dealer omitted.** The paper names FOMO-Dealer under user empathy; SKILL.md omits it.
3. **Unconfirmed cards.** Black Hat, White Hat, Adversary, Ally, Liberator, Transparent Pricer, Consistent Communicator were not found in any page read. Camouflager, Cynic, Two-Faced appear only as candidate roles (Cynic also on a card image). They may be invented, drawn from a different print, or correct; the card figure and Supplemental Material must be checked.
4. **Fourth mode.** The paper and the deck have three action cards (Evaluation, Reverse Brainstorming, Ethical Dialogue). SKILL.md has four modes, adding Conceptualization. Mark it as an edbx addition.
5. **Pairing claims.** `cards.md` says some cards "appear without an explicitly named Hero pair" in the source (Cynic, Black Hat, Puppeteer). The sources read say every card has a Hero on its reverse. The multi-Hero pairs ("Liberator (or Life Coach)") are likely edbx's own.
6. **Author order and title.** SKILL.md cites "Mehta, Chivukula, Gray, et al. (2024)" (correct for the preprint, with Gairola as fourth author). The DIS 2025 version has a different title and the author order Chivukula, Mehta, Gray, Gairola. SKILL.md and cards.md cite only the preprint; THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md mentions "ACM DIS 2025" without a DOI. Add doi:10.1145/3715336.3735776.
7. **Spelling.** The paper (as read) spells "Nickling-and-Diming"; SKILL.md has "Nickeling-and-Diming". Check the printed card.
8. **Illustrative detail attributed to the deck.** Examples such as sad mascots, streak-loss warnings, "hidden cancel paths" and "fake urgency timers" sit inside card descriptions; they are not found in the sources read and look like edbx illustration.
9. **Book profile.** `cards.md` says the deck is "depicted in" a *Universal Methods of Ethical Design* profile; not confirmed.

## Stage I — Imagine: Exclusion Scenario

Select one assumption from Stage C. Describe a specific person being excluded.

**Assumption selected:** "This design assumes the user _____________________."

**Person:** Name, age, relevant characteristics, context

**Setting:** Where are they? What time? What else is happening?

**Task:** What are they trying to accomplish?

**What happens:** Describe the moment of exclusion step by step.

**How it feels:** What does the person experience emotionally?

## Discrepancies

1. **Two different exercises are cited as one method.** SKILL.md says the
   method is "Klassen & Fiesler, 2022; Mauldin, 2018". Mauldin (2018) is the
   product-team "Black Mirror brainstorms" exercise. Klassen & Fiesler (2022)
   study a separate classroom exercise, the "Black Mirror Writers Room", through
   interviews with 12 instructors. The paper does not originate the method
   in the skill.
2. **Economic / political / social lenses.** SKILL.md says the lenses "come
   from the source method". They do not appear in the Mauldin excerpts, the
   Everyday Ethics summary, or the Klassen & Fiesler abstract. Possibly from
   the book; unconfirmed.
3. **Three episodes, three-act arc, 8–12 prompts, 6–10 anti-goals with Trace
   and Test, and "load-bearing" anti-goals** have no source found. They look
   like edbx design choices. Mauldin's exercise builds one episode per team and
   a poster; SKILL.md should mark these as skill additions.
4. **Mauldin steps the skill omits.** Sharing the posters, finding common
   patterns across them, giving feedback, and reflecting on links to current
   work; also the "well-intended idea" framing question and the suggested
   group of 3–4 with about 15 minutes of sketching and 5 minutes to define
   anti-goals.
5. **"Pitch quote".** Mauldin's step is a line a character might say. SKILL.md
   asks for a 2–4 sentence "Netflix-style blurb". Different artefact.
6. **Netflix title-card poster, single self-contained HTML file** is an edbx
   addition; Mauldin's poster is a hand-made team artefact.
7. **Incomplete citation.** "Mauldin, J. (2018). Black mirror brainstorms: a
   product design exercise." omits the venue (UX Collective) and URL, and the
   title wording differs (the article title is "Black Mirror brainstorms — a
   product design exercise"). The book is named without authors, year or
   publisher (Chivukula & Gray, 2025, Rockport).
8. **Long verbatim quote.** The "Thomas" pitch quote is attributed to the
   book. I could not find it online; verify it is the book's text and consider
   replacing it with an original example (copyright).
9. **Book classification line** (Value Posture, Ethical Framework, etc.) is
   unverified and cannot be confirmed without the book.

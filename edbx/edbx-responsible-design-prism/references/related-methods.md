# Related Methods — EDB Toolkit Connections

The Responsible Design Prism is part of the Ethical Design Box (EDB) skillset. It connects to other skills in specific, complementary ways.

---

## Companion Skill Relationships

### edb-motivation-matrix
The Motivation Matrix surfaces *why* users act — mapping five motivational types (Achievement, Social Acceptance, Fear, Power, Incentive) across user contexts. The Prism evaluates *how* design exploits or respects that motivation.
**Sequence:** Run the Motivation Matrix to understand what drives user behavior, then the Prism to assess whether the design weaponizes those drivers.

### edb-anotherlens
Another Lens surfaces the designer's internal biases, assumptions, and worldview. The Prism evaluates the design output those biases produce — the artifact, not the creator.
**Sequence:** Run Another Lens on the design team first, then the Prism on the product. Together they answer: "What biases did we bring, and what did they produce?"

### edb-humane-design-guide
The Humane Design Guide maps how design exploits six human sensitivities (Attention, Emotional, Sensemaking, Decision-making, Social Reasoning, Group Dynamics). The Prism places that exploitation on a responsibility spectrum and generates transition paths.
**Sequence:** Run the Humane Design Guide to identify sensitivity violations, then the Prism to score them on the dark-to-responsible spectrum and generate redesign pathways.

### edb-worrystorming
Worrystorming generates broad ethical concerns about a design before they materialize. The Prism helps prevent those concerns from becoming dark patterns by providing a structured evaluation framework.
**Sequence:** Run Worrystorming first to surface concerns, then the Prism to evaluate whether the current design is trending toward those concerns.

### edb-cider
CIDER audits who is excluded by design through assumption elicitation (Ability, Capacity, Environment, Resources). The Prism evaluates whether that exclusion is deliberate (dark pattern territory) or unintentional (transitional zone).
**Sequence:** Run CIDER to surface exclusion, then the Prism to evaluate the intent and responsibility of that exclusion.

## Future Method Connections

These methods are referenced in the source material and may be added to the EDB skillset:

- **Fair Patterns** — The Prism identifies dark patterns; Fair Patterns provides the alternative pattern library
- **White Hat Design Patterns** — Positive counterpart: what responsible design looks like in practice at the pattern level

## Recommended Sequences

**Full ethical audit:**
1. edb-anotherlens (self-awareness)
2. edb-cider (inclusion check)
3. edb-humane-design-guide (sensitivity audit)
4. edb-responsible-design-prism (responsibility placement)
5. edb-worrystorming (forward-looking concerns)
6. edb-motivation-matrix (motivational mechanics)

**Dark pattern sweep:**
1. edb-responsible-design-prism (identify dark patterns)
2. edb-humane-design-guide (assess sensitivity impact)
3. edb-cider (check for exclusionary assumptions)

**Pre-launch ethical review:**
1. edb-worrystorming
2. edb-humane-design-guide
3. edb-responsible-design-prism

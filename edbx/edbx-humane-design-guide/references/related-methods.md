# Related Methods — EDB Toolkit Connections

The Humane Design Guide is part of the Ethical Design Box (EDB) skillset. It connects to other skills in the toolkit in specific, complementary ways.

---

## Companion Skill Relationships

### edbx-worrystorming
Worrystorming generates free-form ethical concerns about a product. The Humane Design Guide gives those concerns a structured sensitivity taxonomy.
**Sequence:** Run Worrystorming first to generate raw concerns, then use the Humane Design Guide to organize those concerns into the six sensitivity categories and generate structured alternatives.

### edbx-responsible-design-prism
The Prism places a design on the dark-to-responsible spectrum across five axes. The Humane Design Guide provides the six-axis sensitivity framework for diagnosing *what specifically* makes the design irresponsible.
**Sequence:** Run the Humane Design Guide to identify sensitivity violations, then use the Prism to place those violations on the responsibility spectrum and generate redesign pathways.

### edbx-motivation-matrix
The Motivation Matrix maps what motivates users across five motivation types (Achievement, Social Acceptance, Fear, Power, Incentive). The Humane Design Guide maps where that motivation is exploited against users' interests.
**Sequence:** Run the Motivation Matrix to understand motivational mechanics, then the Humane Design Guide to check whether those mechanics exploit human sensitivities.

### edbx-anotherlens
Another Lens surfaces the designer's internal biases and worldview assumptions. The Humane Design Guide audits the product's external impact on human sensitivities.
**Sequence:** Run Another Lens first to check what biases the design team carries, then the Humane Design Guide to see how those biases manifest as sensitivity exploitation in the product.

### edbx-cider
CIDER audits who is excluded by design through assumption elicitation. The Humane Design Guide audits how those who are included are exploited.
**Sequence:** Run CIDER to ensure broad inclusion, then the Humane Design Guide to ensure the included experience is humane.

## Future Method Connections

These methods are referenced in the source material and may be added to the EDB skillset:

- **Maslow Mirrored** — Reframes needs hierarchy; the Humane Design Guide applies those needs as vulnerability axes
- **Well-Being Design Cards** — Positive counterpart: design patterns that actively support each sensitivity

## Recommended Sequences

**Full ethical audit:**
1. edbx-anotherlens (self-awareness)
2. edbx-cider (inclusion check)
3. edbx-humane-design-guide (sensitivity audit)
4. edbx-responsible-design-prism (responsibility placement)
5. edbx-worrystorming (forward-looking concerns)
6. edbx-motivation-matrix (motivational mechanics)

**Quick sensitivity check:**
1. edbx-humane-design-guide
2. edbx-responsible-design-prism

**Pre-launch review:**
1. edbx-worrystorming
2. edbx-humane-design-guide
3. edbx-cider

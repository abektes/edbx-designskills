  "(c) 2026 Center for Humane Technology. All Rights Reserved." with no
  Creative Commons notice, and the Terms of Use page was not read. Treat as
  copyrighted, free to download. Paraphrasing the method: yes, with credit.
  Reproducing the worksheet, its sensitivity wordings or its "inhibited /
  supported / enabled to" lists verbatim: no, without permission. Shipping a
  modified or re-laid-out worksheet: no, without permission; the skill's
  `assets/worksheet-template.md` and `references/six-sensitivities.md` should
  be checked against this at stage B because they carry near-verbatim wordings.
- `silverman2021slides`: a third-party copy of CHT material, no licence; do not
  rely on it for reuse and do not reproduce it.
- `cht2024blog`, `cht2026course`: CHT copyright, no licence stated.

  rights reserved. Paraphrase only.
- `zhou2021maslow`: no licence or copyright notice on the page; unknown.
- `positivecomputing2021cards`: licence not read; unknown.
- Net: the repo may paraphrase the six-sensitivity method with CHT credit. No
  source here allows copying the worksheet text or shipping a modified copy
  in a paid bundle; none was found to forbid commercial use in so many words,
  but the CHT site is all rights reserved and the guide's own terms are unknown.

## Discrepancies

Contradicted by the source (I read a source and it says otherwise; the source
is a third-party slide that credits CHT, not the official PDF):

1. "Enabled to ..." wordings. Slide, Attention: "bring more focus and
   mindfulness" (skill: "find focus and peace"). Sensemaking: "consider, learn,
   express and feel grounded" (skill: "find truth and clarity"). Decision-making:
   "gain agency, purpose, and mobilization of intent" (skill: "act with agency
   and control"). Social Reasoning: "connect more safely and authentically with
   others" (skill: "connect meaningfully"). Group Dynamics: "develop a sense of
   belonging and cooperation" (skill: "build strong communities"). Emotional is
   unclear on the slide (columns misaligned); the skill's "process and adapt"
   is not on it.
2. Inhibition wording: the slide says Group Dynamics are inhibited when people
   are "excluded, divided and mobilized through fear" (skill: "managed through
   fear"); Sensemaking when information is "fear-based, out of context,
   confusing, or manipulative" (skill: "misleading").
3. Worksheet shape. The slide's sheet 1 has the product, value proposition and
   measure of success, then "inhibited when / supported when" per
   sensitivity, then a 1-6 ranking; sheet 2 is an action statement. The skill's
   four-column worksheet (current state, what's at risk, red/amber/green level,
   improvement) and its "six rows, four columns" claim are not the original
   structure; the skill presents it as the equivalent output.
4. Order: the slide lists Emotional first; the skill lists Attention first.
   Cosmetic, but the skill calls it the guide's order.

5. Publication date and named authors of the guide: not found. CHT blog and

6. "Method #47" and the hashtags: book unread; method list pages do not carry

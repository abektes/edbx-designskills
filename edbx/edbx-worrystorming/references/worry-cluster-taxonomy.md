# Worry Cluster Taxonomy

Full definitions of all eight concern categories with example worries for each. Use this reference when generating worries in Step 2.

---

## 🗃️ Data & Privacy

How is user data collected, stored, shared, and used? Who has access? Is consent meaningful?

**Example worries:**
- What if user data is sold to third parties without meaningful consent?
- What if the data collection scope expands beyond what users agreed to?
- What if a breach exposes sensitive personal information?
- What if the product collects data about non-users (bystanders)?
- What if data is used to build profiles that could be used against users in other contexts (insurance, employment, legal)?
- What if users cannot easily delete their data?
- What if the product operates in jurisdictions with different privacy expectations?

---

## 🌍 Environmental Impact

What is the ecological cost of this product — in energy, materials, waste, and infrastructure?

**Example worries:**
- What is the carbon footprint of running this service at scale?
- What e-waste does the hardware dependency create?
- What energy consumption does continuous data processing require?
- What happens to the environmental cost when user base grows 10x?
- Does this product replace something more sustainable, or add to total consumption?
- Are there supply chain impacts (mining for device components, server manufacturing)?

---

## 👥 Community & Social

How does this product affect communities, social structures, and collective values?

**Example worries:**
- What communal values or practices does this disrupt?
- Who is excluded from participation due to access, language, or cultural barriers?
- What happens to local economies or social structures when this product scales?
- Does this product replace community-based services with centralized ones?
- What social dynamics change when this product mediates relationships?
- Does this product assume an individualistic culture that may not translate globally?

---

## 💸 Economic

Who profits from this product, who bears the cost, and does it widen economic inequality?

**Example worries:**
- Who makes money from this and who is exploited in the value chain?
- Does this product widen the gap between those who have access and those who don't?
- What happens to people whose livelihoods are displaced by this product?
- Are the costs (financial, temporal, emotional) distributed fairly across users?
- Does the pricing model exploit urgency, necessity, or vulnerability?
- What happens when a user can no longer afford the product — what do they lose?

---

## 🔒 Safety & Harm

Who could be physically, psychologically, or materially harmed by this product, and under what conditions?

**Example worries:**
- What failure modes exist that could cause harm (outages, errors, bad recommendations)?
- Who is most vulnerable to harm from this product?
- What happens during edge cases — emergencies, system failures, unexpected inputs?
- Could this product be weaponized or used for harassment?
- What happens to users who depend on this product when it changes or shuts down?
- Are there populations for whom product failure is catastrophic rather than inconvenient?

---

## 🧠 Psychological

Does this product affect users' mental health, cognitive function, or emotional wellbeing?

**Example worries:**
- Does this exploit cognitive biases (loss aversion, sunk cost, social proof)?
- Does this product create anxiety, shame, or compulsive behavior?
- What is the impact on users with existing mental health conditions?
- Does this product encourage comparison, competition, or performance anxiety?
- Does the design create dependency or withdrawal effects?
- What happens to users' self-perception when they engage with this product over time?

---

## ⚖️ Power & Fairness

Who has control, who doesn't, and who is silenced or marginalized by this product's existence?

**Example worries:**
- Who has control over the product's decisions and direction?
- Who is silenced or unable to provide feedback?
- What power asymmetries does this product create or reinforce?
- Does this product centralize power in ways that reduce user agency?
- Who benefits from opacity in the product's operations?
- Can users understand, challenge, or override the product's decisions?
- Does this product create or reinforce systemic inequalities?

---

## 🔮 Future Consequences

What happens when this product scales, is copied by competitors, or becomes embedded in daily life?

**Example worries:**
- What happens at 10x or 100x scale — do the harms amplify?
- What precedent does this product set for the industry?
- What happens when competitors copy this approach?
- How does this product change norms and expectations over time?
- What does the world look like in 5 years if this product succeeds?
- What unintended uses might emerge as the user base diversifies?
- What dependencies does this create — what happens if the product disappears?

  copyright page was not readable, so the licence is unknown; assume all
  rights reserved. Paraphrase the method idea (generate worries, cluster,
  reframe as values): yes, ideas are not covered. Reproduce the entry text,
  its illustration, its hashtag list as a set, or its examples: no. Ship a
  modified version of the method: yes, since SKILL.md is an adaptation and
  adds much that is not in the book (rows 9–14); keep the attribution to the
  compilation and say what edbx added. Stage B must compare SKILL.md wording
  against p. 208 and reword anything close; the Overview and the two
  opening paragraphs are the likeliest places for close paraphrase.

  its text were copied into the repo, the non-commercial and share-alike terms
  would conflict with a paid bundle; paraphrase only.

## Discrepancies

Contradicted by the source (read the source and it says otherwise): none. The
entry text was not readable, so nothing could be contradicted.

Not found in a readable source (the full source could not be read, or no
source contains it):

- N1. Everything in rows 3–8 depends on the book entry (p. 208), which could not
  be read. Only the method's name, number and page are confirmed.
- N2. The eight-category taxonomy, severity tags, counts of worries and
  clusters, time budgets and facilitation steps (rows 9–14) have no source. If
  the book entry does not contain them, they are edbx additions and
  SKILL.md should say so alongside the existing "edbx adaptation" note
  (which currently covers only the Evidence Ledger).
- N3. SKILL.md says Worrystorming "modifies traditional brainstorming" and ends
  with a "generate refined ideas" step. Neither can be checked against the
  book entry.
- N4. SKILL.md calls it an "ethical pre-mortem". The pre-mortem technique has a
  separate origin that SKILL.md does not cite (not checked here); the book
  entry may or may not use the term.
- N5. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` credits "Universal Methods of Ethical Design,
  Method #100" without authors. The authors are Chivukula and Gray (Rockport,
  2025); the notice should name them. The "Method #100" number and title are
  correct.
- N6. `references/related-methods.md` lists Empathic Walkthrough and Moral Agent
  as "referenced in the source material"; that statement is unchecked
  (row 5).

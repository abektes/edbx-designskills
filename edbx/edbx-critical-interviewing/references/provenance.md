# Provenance — edbx-critical-interviewing

Stage A draft (sources and licences, no downloads, 2026-10-13). One row per
element the skill attributes to a source. Locator and anchor are filled only
where confirmed from a page read in this pass (publisher table of contents,
Crossref, repository record); otherwise Locator is `to verify` and the anchor
is a best guess marked `(guess)`.

**Main finding:** the method's primary source is Carspecken (1996), a
book. I could not find a published description of a "Critical Interviewing"
design method with lead-off, back-up and emergency questions, covert
categories and construct-building probes. Those terms appear in none of the
papers and pages I could read. The most likely bridge from Carspecken to design
ethics is the Gray / Chivukula research group's "critical interview"
approach; it needs Stage B checking against the papers' method sections.

## Discrepancies

1. **Method name and protocol terms have no traceable source.** "Critical
   Interviewing" as a named design method, and its lead-off / back-up /
   emergency question set, covert categories, construct-building probes, were
   not found in any page read. The Routledge table of contents does not
   contain these terms (chapter titles only). SKILL.md says the method "was
   developed from Carspecken's (1996) framework and adapted for design
   research contexts" without naming who adapted it. Likely adapters are the
   Gray / Chivukula group; unconfirmed.
2. **Internal inconsistency.** "Critical Interviewing operates in five modes"
   but three modes are listed (Protocol Design, Live Interview Support,
   Post-Interview Analysis).
3. **"Example covert categories from the source".** The four examples (role of
   ethics, awareness of ethical role, drivers of sensitivity, personal design
   values) are unattributed. Row 11 offers a candidate (`chivukula2020dimensions`)
   but the paper's text was not read.
4. **Legal framing.** "4/5 / 80% rule under Title VII": the four-fifths rule
   is in agency guidelines (29 C.F.R. § 1607.4(D)) and is a presumption, not
   a Title VII statutory threshold, and it applies to employment selection
   procedures. GDPR Art. 22 and AI Act Arts. 5/6 are named without text
   checked; verify the scope of each (Art. 22 concerns solely automated
   decisions).
5. **Sections F to J (domain bank, sequencing, harms inventory, guardrails,
   halt checklist)** and the "minimum output" list are edbx additions. No
   source found. Mode 1 also assigns Mode 1 letters A to J while the
   Output Format section shows only A to E components; internal mismatch.
6. **Sequencing rule** ("users before engineers before executives") is stated
   as fact with no source.
7. **No citation list in SKILL.md.** The only source named is "Carspecken
   (1996)" with no title, publisher or the design-research adapters.
8. **Search-tool conflict.** One search summary said the CHI 2020 paper cites
   Carspecken; the Crossref reference list shows no Carspecken entry for
   `chivukula2020dimensions`, `gray2019ethical` or `chivukula2021identity`.
   Only `gray2016meaning` does. Resolve in Stage B by reading the methods
   sections.
9. **Related-skills line** ("Critical Interviewing ... values interview",
   "designer values research") is trigger wording, not a source claim.

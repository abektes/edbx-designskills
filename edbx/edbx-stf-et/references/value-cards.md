# Value Explainer Cards

Source: Stanford McCoy Family Center for Ethics in Society — Ethics Toolkit V1.1, 2025
License: CC BY 4.0 — ethicstoolkit.stanford.edu

Value Cards are actionable guides to understand values and make them tangible. Each card provides a clear definition and three observable spectra — each spectrum shows what degradation and promotion of the value looks like in practice.

Use these cards throughout the stf-et chain:
- **Impacts Explorer** — to name the values impacted by ripple effects
- **Ethics Frame** — to define values driving your work and assess how they are impacted
- **Ethics Gauge** — the Fairness and Empowerment dimensions draw directly on these cards
- **Weighing Options** — to articulate what each option prioritizes

---

## WELL-BEING
*The flourishing of individuals and society, including health, happiness, and growth.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it affect well-being?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Our creation contributes to physical or mental harm, promoting unhealthy habits or conditions that worsen well-being. | Our creation enhances physical and mental health, fostering habits and conditions that support well-being. |
| Our creation fosters dissatisfaction, stress, or discomfort, leading to a decline in overall happiness. | Our creation cultivates joy and fulfillment, enhancing overall happiness and well-being. |
| Our creation stifles personal development or growth, keeping individuals stagnant or preventing progress. | Our creation encourages personal growth, learning, and empowerment, helping individuals reach their full potential. |

## JUSTICE
*The fair distribution of benefits and burdens of (social) goods.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it contribute to justice?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Certain groups are more negatively impacted by the effects of your actions/creation. | There are no disproportionately impacted people or groups. |
| (The success of) our creation would cause or perpetuate a bias or the marginalization of certain people or groups. | (The success of) our creation would reduce current bias or marginalization. |
| Those who are harmed are also more marginalized in society. Those who benefit hold power and have privilege. | Those who are currently marginalized or have the greatest need benefit the most. |

## TRUST
*Optimistic (and voluntary) reliance on the competence and goodwill of another with respect to our interests.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it influence trust?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| We do not consider the key needs, interests, and expectations of those impacted by our creation. | We proactively take into account the key needs, interests, and expectations of those impacted by our creation. |
| People's expectations don't properly match the limitations and weaknesses (and the benefits) of our creation and the values driving us. | We are properly managing people's expectations about our actions and creation, and take responsibility for doing so. |
| There are insufficient ways for those affected by our creation to be heard and keep us accountable. | There are effective ways for those affected by our creation to be heard and keep us accountable. |

---

## PRIVACY
*The flow of personal information which supports the individual's right to control or protection from unwanted intrusion.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it impact privacy?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Personal or meaningful information is exposed to inappropriate parties or the public. | No personal or meaningful data is collected, or sufficient precautions are taken to avoid exposure and unwanted use. |
| Once personal data is shared, people lose the ability to revoke access or control over how their data is used. | There is ample ability for people to revoke sharing or use permissions, without penalty, and to control how their data is used. |
| If our creation is used broadly (beyond its immediate scope) or widely, expectations of privacy would be reduced. | If our creation is used broadly or widely, expectations of privacy would be increased. |

## DIGNITY
*The inherent worth that every person possesses equally, which deserves recognition and respect.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it preserve dignity?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Our creation manipulates, coerces, or misleads people, or *allows others to*. | Our creation does not coerce, manipulate, or mislead and limits others' ability to do so. |
| Our creation works for people with particular abilities and backgrounds, and shuts out others. | Diverse needs, abilities, and cultural backgrounds are adequately and conscientiously accommodated. |
| People are treated as means for our needs, and exploited through the use of our creation, or the conditions it creates. | Our actions and creations are designed to serve the needs of people, and ultimately empowers them. |

## VIRTUES
*Habits or dispositions that guide us to act in morally good ways.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it relate to virtues?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Our creation discourages virtuous behaviors (integrity, empathy, generosity…) and promotes decay and personal degradation. | Our creation promotes virtuous behaviors and self-improvement and personal growth. |
| Our creation removes opportunities to exercise virtues and/or makes it more costly to do so. | Our actions and creation creates opportunities to exercise virtues, or makes it easier to do so. |
| Our creation discourages reflection on the importance of one's own actions, and discourages people from taking responsibility. | Our creation encourages reflection on the importance of one's own actions, and encourages people to take responsibility. |

---

## AUTONOMY
*The ability to govern oneself by one's own judgment.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it impact autonomy?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Our creation/actions make decisions for people that should be up to them. | Our actions and creation allow people to make meaningful and consequential choices for themselves. |
| Our creation encourages misuse, overuse, or dependence. (It becomes difficult or costly to disengage.) | Our creation promotes responsible use and independence. |
| If used widely, our creation would make it hard for people to opt out. | Widespread use of our creation would bolster autonomy and preserve ability to easily opt out. |

---

## RESPONSIBILITY
*Awareness, care, and accountability regarding the effects of one's choices and actions.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it exercise responsibility?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| There are limited ways to monitor misuse of our creation, or the negative impacts it can create. | There are sufficient ways to monitor misuse of our creation, or the negative impacts it can create. |
| There are limited ways to take action on misuse of our creation or its negative impacts. | There are effective ways to take action on misuse of our creation or its negative impacts. |
| There is no commitment to act to mitigate negative impacts of our actions/creation. | There is a commitment to act to mitigate negative impacts of our creation, even when used broadly and widely, and reasonable expectations of action. |

---

## RELATIONSHIPS
*A social association, connection, or affiliation between two or more persons, which provides goods that cannot be enjoyed outside of relationships.*

Considering your action/creations … **How might it impact interpersonal relationships?**

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| When using our creation, people understand, communicate and connect poorly with one another. | When using our creation, people understand, communicate and connect well with one another. |
| Negative attitudes and behaviors (e.g., conflict, polarization, isolation) appear or worsen among people. | Positive attitudes and behaviors (e.g., reciprocity, empathy, community) appear or improve among people. |
| The way our actions and creation alter how people relate to or interact with others degrades the goods of their relationships. | The way our actions and creation alter how people relate to or interact with others preserves or enhances the goods of their relationships. |

## Creating Your Own Value Card

Use this structure to define additional values specific to your work or context. Custom cards can cover moral values not listed above, non-moral organizational values (efficiency, user-centeredness, environmental sustainability), or domain-specific principles (legal standards, humanitarian principles, field-specific codes of ethics).

Each custom card should include:
1. **Name** — the value, in a word or phrase
2. **Definition** — a clear sentence defining what the value means (need not be philosophical; put words to the idea concretely)
3. **Three spectra** — each spectrum identifies a key dimension of the value and shows what degradation and promotion look like in observable scenarios

**Template:**

```
## [VALUE NAME]
*[Definition sentence.]*

Considering your action/creations … How might it affect [value name]?

| DEGRADING | PROMOTING |
|-----------|-----------|
| Observable scenario showing value degraded | Observable scenario showing value promoted |
| Observable scenario showing value degraded | Observable scenario showing value promoted |
| Observable scenario showing value degraded | Observable scenario showing value promoted |
```

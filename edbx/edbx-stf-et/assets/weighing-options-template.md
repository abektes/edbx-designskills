# Weighing Options Worksheet
*Use this tool to make ethical choices by weighing considerations of different courses of action.*

Stanford McCoy Family Center for Ethics in Society — Ethics Toolkit
Source: ethicstoolkit.stanford.edu | CC BY 4.0

---

**Instructions:** Begin by describing the current situation. Then outline two or more courses of action and assess their societal impact, organizational impact, obstacles, and value alignment — completing all sections for one option at a time, or working row by row across all options. Consider the most significant advantages and disadvantages of each option, how those align with what matters — to you and to those impacted — and then select an option to pursue or note what else you need to learn.

## Licence

  licence terms; the template is free to download. Treat as all rights
  reserved, free to download, no explicit permission to redistribute or modify.
  Paraphrasing the six-step method is fine. Do not reproduce the worksheet,
  template layout, step text or images verbatim in the paid bundle; do not ship a
  modified copy of the template. The skill's own text (tension map, red lines,
  audit, risk register) is not covered. Whether the name "Ethical Contract" is
  restricted: unknown.
- **`carlock2022contract`.** No licence stated; it restates Gispen. Same
  paraphrase-only treatment.
- **`chivukula2021surveying`.** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: non-commercial and
  share-alike. Not copied into the repo; cite only.
- **`eeoc1978ugesp`.** US federal regulation text; check the page for any
  notice. The numerical rule can be cited with attribution.
- **`chivukula2025universal`.** Commercial book; paraphrase only.
- **Blockers.** No source forbids commercial use outright, but Gispen's
  all-rights-reserved default blocks verbatim reproduction and modified
  distribution of her worksheet and wording. The CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 paper blocks
  copying its text into a paid bundle. The skill currently copies neither.

## Discrepancies

1. **Step count.** SKILL.md says "five steps"; Gispen's method has six (explain
   the disclaimer, define themes, divide responsibilities, three objectives,
   design goal, sign). The skill merges steps 2-3 and re-purposes step 1.
2. **Missing input.** The source requires a completed Ethical Disclaimer
   before the contract; the skill's Inputs do not list it (only "See Also"
   does). Its Inputs name Worrystorming, Value Dams and Pledge Works, which
   the source does not mention.
3. **No creator credit.** SKILL.md names no author or year. Jet Gispen, 2017,
   is not credited, and the source asks for no stated reuse terms either way.
4. **Four-part objectives, quantitative thresholds, named owners, review
   cadence.** Not in the source. Source says only "three main ethical
   objectives". Skill's own extension.
5. **Step 1.5, Red Lines, Step 3.5 Bias & Harm Audit, Commitment Risk Register,
   Renewal Triggers, role table** are the skill's additions; none appear in
   the source.
6. **"Industry standard floor: 0.8 / four-fifths rule".** The rule comes from
   a US employment-selection regulation (29 CFR 1607.4(D)), where it is a
   screening benchmark, not a standard for product features. "Industry
   standard" and the applicability to digital products are unsupported. The
   n >= 30 and 0.7 confidence figures have no source and should stay labelled
   examples.
7. **Design goal.** Source updates an existing design goal that stakeholders
   agree; the skill generates a new unifying statement from a fixed template.
8. **Signatures.** The skill says signatures make this "distinct from informal
   pledges"; the source does not compare with pledges.
9. **Name spelling.** Carlock's page spells the author "Gipsen" in one place and
   "Gispen" in links; the toolkit's own spelling is "Gispen".
10. **Time.** The source suggests 30-45 minutes; the skill states none, which is
    fine but the skill's added steps would likely exceed it.

- D2: Impact analysis covers effects on people AND on society, and for those effects says which related ethical values are promoted or degraded (row 7).

- D6: Imagines impacts by looking back from the future (a retrospective account written from a later vantage point) (row 5). Scored in any output where the prompt asks for Future Story or the output offers a forward-looking impact account; otherwise scored `no` with reason "not run".

- M2 (misstated method): wrong creator or institution (not Stanford's McCoy Family Center / Travaglianti & Both), wrong number or names of the tools (the page lists five tools plus Values Explainer Cards), or a tool described contrary to the page's one-line description (e.g. Weighing Options not about comparing options, Ethics Gauge not granular). Unverifiable internals (sections, dimensions, card names) are not scored here.
